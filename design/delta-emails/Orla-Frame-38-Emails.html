Why the confirmation carries a details block and the invites don't
It's the only receipt a waitlisted vendor gets — there's no dashboard to check — so the four facts we hold are listed and a reply is offered as the correction channel. The invites need no receipt; they need one button.
No dates, no counts, no urgency
No “within X days”, no expiring invite, no position, no “you're one of N”. If the invite link ever does expire, that belongs in the link's failure screen, not in a sentence here that would be a timing promise.
One clay button, no second action
Both invites carry exactly one, and the confirmation carries none — the reply line is the action, and a Visit Orla button under “nothing else you need to do” would contradict the sentence above it.
Plain-text fallback is the same sentences
Each send is short enough to be its own plain-text part verbatim, button becoming a bare URL. Nothing here depends on the card, the mark or the rule to make sense.
