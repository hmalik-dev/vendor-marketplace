A signed-in customer cannot act on a vendor pitch — one account cannot be both roles — so the trust band runs straight into the footer. A signed-in vendor never reaches this page at all; they are redirected to /dashboard. Account column swaps Sign in / Sign up for the customer’s own links.
Why the two-audience fork went
The customer half pointed at Find a vendor — a button whose only job was scrolling back up to the hero’s live search bar. The vendor half said Go to your dashboard to a visitor with no dashboard. What replaces both is vendor-only, and it is the page’s only supply-side entry besides one nav link.
Stacked, not columned — and flush left
A left-aligned two-column version left roughly 500px of dead ink on the right. Pitch and controls hold the top line edge to edge, a hairline crosses the full width, and the steps run as equal thirds. No centred inner column anywhere — the band and footer sit on the same 40px gutter as every block above them.
No pricing figures here
An earlier pass had 12%, $0 to list and the payout interval as display numerals. Commission is a conversion number, and with no room to frame it the first money fact a vendor meets is what gets deducted. Worse, customers read this page too — “Orla takes 12%” suggests a vendor costs more here than direct, undercutting No service fee above.
The footer drops one step of ink
Band #23201C, footer #1C1916. Two masses of the same ink split by a hairline read as one ~400px dark region; a step down lets the band be the last piece of content and the footer be furniture. One new ink value, no new hue — and the old compensating border-top comes off. Muted text steps #A79D8C on the band to #8C8375 on the footer; micro-labels are #8C8375 at 4.68:1 (#7A7266 was tried and fails).
Legal row, not a fifth column
Terms · Privacy · Cookies and the copyright sit on a thin line under the grid, behind a rgba(248,245,239,.1) hairline. A fifth column would give three legal links the same visual weight as Browse. Dashboard is dropped from the signed-out Account column — a visitor has no dashboard — and the signed-in column carries My bookings · Messages · Settings · Sign out instead.
