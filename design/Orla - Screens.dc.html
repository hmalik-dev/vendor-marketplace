What I'd do next
Three things are stubbed rather than designed: the review submission flow, the packages editor, and the Stripe payout onboarding — all straightforward once these patterns are set. Note on public metrics: the landing page, sign-up panel and category cards carry no platform statistics — no vendor counts, no "events booked", no average rating. A new marketplace has none of those honestly, and a placeholder number is worse than none.
