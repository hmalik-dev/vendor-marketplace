# Stock photography credits

Every photograph in this directory, where it came from, and under what licence.

The [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license) grants free commercial use
with no attribution required. It is recorded anyway: "no attribution required"
is not the same as "no provenance", and the question a launch review asks is
"where did this come from", which a licence name alone does not answer.

## The unverified rows are a launch blocker

Six of these seven arrived with no record of where they came from, so nobody
can currently say whether the project is licensed to publish them. That is a
question to settle before the marketplace is public, not after — a takedown
lands on the landing page and the auth screens at once.

Resolving one means either finding its origin and filling in the row, or
replacing it with a photograph whose origin is known. `auth.jpg` is the worked
example: sourced deliberately, recorded here in the same commit.

`vendors/` and `marketing/` are not covered by this file yet and need the same
pass.
