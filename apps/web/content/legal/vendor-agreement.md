## Listing and accuracy

You are responsible for what appears on your profile: the prices, the availability, the images and the description of what a customer receives. You confirm you hold the rights to every image you upload and that you are permitted to trade in the places you list.

## Accepting a booking

A request reaches you with a date, a price and a scope. It is live for {{requestExpiryDays}}. If you do not answer, it expires and nothing happens. Once you accept it, it is a contract between you and that customer for the date, price and scope shown on it.

## What the customer pays, and when you are paid

The customer pays {{brand}} at checkout, and {{brand}} holds the money until the event has happened. Your share is released {{payoutReleaseHours}} after the event date. Neither you nor the customer can bring that forward. It then reaches your bank on Stripe's payout schedule.

## Commission

{{brand}} retains {{commission}} of each booking, deducted when the payout is released. There is no monthly fee and no listing fee. {{brand}} adds no fee on top of your published price. The customer pays exactly what you published.

## If a booking is canceled

If the customer cancels, their refund follows the schedule shown to them at checkout. Whatever is not refunded is released to you on the usual schedule. If **you** cancel a confirmed booking, the customer is refunded in full and no commission is retained.

## If something is disputed

A customer who reports a problem pauses the release while we look at it. If a booking has to be put right after the payout has run, you return your share and {{brand}} returns its commission.

## Your prices and your taxes

You set your prices and you keep them accurate. You are responsible for your own taxes on what you earn, and for any license or insurance your trade requires. {{brand}} is not your employer, and this agreement does not make it one.

## Payouts and Stripe

Payouts run through Stripe Connect. Stripe collects your bank details and enough identification to pay you legally. {{brand}} never sees them. You are also bound by Stripe's connected-account terms.

## Files you upload

You keep ownership of your photographs and files. You give {{brand}} a license to store them and show them on your profile and in search results, and nothing wider. The license for a file ends when you take it down.

## Ending this agreement

You can unpublish your listing at any time. Bookings you have already accepted still stand. {{brand}} can end this agreement where the law, a payment processor or a serious breach requires it.

## Changes to this agreement

A new version does not replace what you accepted. You are asked to accept each new version before you take further bookings. Your record shows which version you agreed to and when.

That record also holds the IP address your acceptance came from and the browser that sent it. It is not editable and not removable while your account exists.
