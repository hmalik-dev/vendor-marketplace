{{brand}} uses two cookies. Both keep you signed in. That is the whole notice.

| Cookie                             | Set by    | Purpose                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `__Secure-neon-auth.session_token` | Neon Auth | Strictly necessary — your sign-in.                                                                                             |
| `session-revoke-marker`            | {{brand}} | Strictly necessary — set when you end other devices or change your password, so this device stays signed in. Lasts 20 minutes. |

We set no cookies beyond those two and load no advertising or session-recording scripts. We load Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies. A strictly necessary cookie needs no consent, so you will not see a banner. If we ever add anything that stores data on your device or follows you between sites, this page changes first and the banner arrives with it.
