## Who we are

## What {{brand}} does

{{brand}} is a marketplace. Vendors publish what they charge and when they are free; customers book them and pay through the platform. We are not a party to the booking itself and we do not supply the service — the contract for the photography, the catering or the venue is between the customer and the vendor.

What we do provide is the booking record, the payment mechanism, the messaging thread and the dispute route described in section 5.

## Your account

You need an account to book or to be booked, you must be 18 or older, and the details on it must be accurate. Accounts are personal — keep your sign-in to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has it.

To close your account, ask us through Contact support and we will do it. We keep booking and payment records after closure where tax and accounting law requires it; the retention periods are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Bookings and payment

A request you send a vendor is live for {{requestExpiryDays}}. If they have not answered by then it expires on its own and nothing is charged. A booking exists once a vendor accepts your request and you have paid.

Payments are processed by Stripe; {{brand}} never sees or stores your card details. The price you see is the price you pay — there is no service fee added at checkout.

:::panel
Your payment is **held by {{brand}}** until the event has happened. It is released to the vendor {{payoutReleaseHours}} after the event date — a fixed window on the calendar, never something the vendor can trigger early. {{brand}} retains a **{{commission}} commission** on the released amount. Nothing is charged to the customer on top of the vendor's published price.
:::

## Cancellations and refunds

Because the payment is held rather than paid out, a cancellation before the event is a refund rather than a claw-back. The schedule that applies to your booking is shown to you at checkout, before you pay, resolved into that booking's own dates and amounts. It is not restated here, because two copies of a schedule drift apart.

If the **vendor** cancels, you are refunded in full regardless of timing and no commission is retained. If something goes wrong at the event itself, open the booking and use Contact support — a dispute pauses the release while we look at it, and once the release has already run, putting it right means the vendor returns their share and {{brand}} returns its commission.
