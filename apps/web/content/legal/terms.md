## What {{brand}} does

{{brand}} is a marketplace. Vendors publish what they charge and when they are free; customers book them and pay through the platform. We are not a party to the booking and do not supply the service. The contract for the photography, the catering or the venue is between the customer and the vendor.

We provide the booking record, the payment mechanism, the messaging thread and the dispute route in section 5.

To close your account, ask us through Contact support and we will do it. We keep booking and payment records after closure where tax and accounting law requires it. The retention periods are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Bookings and payment

A request you send a vendor is live for {{requestExpiryDays}}. If they do not answer by then, it expires and nothing is charged. A booking exists once a vendor accepts your request and you have paid.

Stripe processes payments. {{brand}} never sees or stores your card details. The price you see is the price you pay, with no service fee added at checkout.

:::panel
Your payment is **held by {{brand}}** until the event has happened. It is released to the vendor {{payoutReleaseHours}} after the event date, and the vendor cannot release it early. {{brand}} retains a **{{commission}} commission** on the released amount. Nothing is charged to the customer on top of the vendor's published price.
:::

## Cancellations and refunds

A cancellation before the event is refunded from the held payment. The refund schedule for your booking is shown at checkout, before you pay, in that booking's own dates and amounts.

If the **vendor** cancels, you are refunded in full regardless of timing and no commission is retained. If something goes wrong at the event, open the booking and use Contact support. A dispute pauses the release while we look at it. If the release has already run and the booking has to be put right, the vendor returns their share and {{brand}} returns its commission.
